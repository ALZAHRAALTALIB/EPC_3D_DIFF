## TL;DR

**EPC-3D-Diff** is a conditional 3D latent diffusion framework for
CBCT-to-CT synthesis.

Its central idea is to enforce **projection-domain rotational
equivariance** during training: an in-plane rotation of the generated
volume should correspond to the appropriate angular shift in its
projections.

The model performs diffusion in a compact learned 3D latent space,
while the physics-based constraint encourages anatomically and
measurement-consistent synthetic CT.

</HighlightedSection>

<PaperSection title="Abstract">

Cone-beam CT (CBCT) is routinely acquired during radiotherapy for
patient setup, but its quantitative reliability is degraded by
scatter, noise, and reconstruction artifacts, limiting Hounsfield
Unit (HU) accuracy.

We propose **EPC-3D-Diff**, a conditional 3D latent diffusion
framework for volumetric CBCT-to-CT synthesis that introduces a
projection-domain equivariance loss derived from acquisition
physics.

The model is evaluated on paired head CBCT/CT phantom data and
paired clinical data, including single-domain and mixed-domain
training, ablations, and comparisons with CycleGAN and conditional
diffusion.

</PaperSection>

<PaperSection title="Why CBCT → CT?">

CBCT is widely used for image-guided radiotherapy, but scatter,
beam hardening, truncation, detector effects, and reconstruction
limitations can reduce HU accuracy and soft-tissue fidelity.

The goal is to learn a mapping

$$
x_{\mathrm{CBCT}}
\longrightarrow
\hat{x}_{\mathrm{CT}}
$$

that preserves anatomy while improving quantitative CT fidelity.

</PaperSection>

<PaperSection title="Method">

<Wide>
  <Picture src={method} alt="EPC-3D-Diff methodology" />
</Wide>

The framework contains three main ideas:

1. **Conditional 3D latent diffusion** for volumetric synthesis.
2. **A lightweight 3D autoencoder** that reduces the spatial
   resolution of the diffusion representation while preserving
   axial depth.
3. **Projection-equivariant physics consistency** derived from
   the rotational symmetry of the CT acquisition operator.

</PaperSection>

<PaperSection title="Latent Diffusion">

Let $\mathcal{E}$ and $\mathcal{D}$ denote the encoder and decoder.
The CT and CBCT volumes are mapped into latent representations:

$$
z_0 = \mathcal{E}_{\psi}(x_0),
\qquad
z_c = \mathcal{E}_{\psi}(x_c).
$$

The diffusion process is performed in the latent space:

$$
z_t =
\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}z_0
+
\sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}\epsilon,
\qquad
\epsilon\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I).
$$

A conditional 3D U-Net predicts the injected noise using the noisy
latent, timestep, and CBCT latent representation.

</PaperSection>

<PaperSection title="Projection-Equivariant Physics Consistency">

For an in-plane rotation $R_{\phi}$, the circular acquisition
geometry gives the approximate equivariance relationship

$$
A_0(R_{\phi}\hat{x}_0)(\eta)
=
A_0(\hat{x}_0)(\eta-\phi).
$$

This motivates the projection-domain loss

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{eq}}
=
\sum_{i=1}^{N_{\phi}}
\left\|
T_{\phi_i}(y_0)
-
A_0R_{\phi_i}(\hat{x}_0)
\right\|_2^2.
$$

The total training objective combines the diffusion objective,
voxel-level reconstruction, edge and Laplacian losses, and the
physics-consistency term:

$$
\mathcal{L} =
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{DDPM}}
+
\lambda_1\mathcal{L}_{L_1}
+
\lambda_2\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{edge}}
+
\lambda_3\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{lap}}
+
\lambda_{\mathrm{eq}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{eq}}.
$$

The important distinction is that the physics operator is used
during training; inference does not require projection operations.

</PaperSection>

<PaperSection title="Inference">

At test time, only the CBCT input is available.

```text
CBCT
  │
  ▼
3D encoder
  │
  ▼
CBCT latent
  │
  ▼
Conditional DDIM sampling
  │
  ▼
Predicted CT latent
  │
  ▼
3D decoder
  │
  ▼
Synthetic CT
```

The paper uses deterministic DDIM sampling with 100 steps for
accelerated inference.

</PaperSection>

<PaperSection title="Training and Inference Cost">

The reported implementation uses two NVIDIA A5000 GPUs, a latent
autoencoder with $C=4$, a conditional 3D U-Net with base width
64, 1000 diffusion steps during training, and 2500 training epochs.

Projection-domain equivariance is applied every 10 epochs using
two random rotations per sample.

At inference, 100-step DDIM sampling is used. No projection
operations are required during inference, and the reported
runtime is approximately **58 seconds per patient**.

</PaperSection>

<PaperSection title="Conclusion">

We presented **EPC-3D-Diff**, a projection-equivariant 3D
conditional latent diffusion framework for CBCT-to-CT synthesis.

The central contribution is to use acquisition physics as a
structural inductive bias during training. Rotational consistency
is enforced in the projection domain, while diffusion is performed
in a compact 3D latent space.

The reported experiments show improved fidelity and robustness
across phantom and clinical data, including mixed-domain training.

</PaperSection>
